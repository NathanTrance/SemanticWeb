\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

This report presents a five-star Linked Open Data application about cocktails,
spirits and distilleries. We design DrinkOnto, an OWL~2 ontology that models
cocktails, their ingredient usages with measures, the spirits and brands
involved, and the distilleries that produce them. A reproducible Python
pipeline collects 441 cocktails from TheCocktailDB and 865 distilleries from
Wikidata, transforms them into an RDF instance graph, and reconciles the
entities against Wikidata and DBpedia, producing more than 1{,}600
\texttt{owl:sameAs} links. The merged graph is exposed through a zero-install
SPARQL endpoint with a preset query interface and dereferenceable URIs, and the
demonstration queries were verified against Apache Jena Fuseki. Link precision
is estimated on a hand-checked sample and reported as [XX\%]; the report closes
with the precision--recall trade-off, limitations and future work.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Linked Open Data; RDF; OWL~2; SPARQL; knowledge
graph; Wikidata; DBpedia.
