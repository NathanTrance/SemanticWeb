\section{Abstract}   
Photometric stereo is a powerful technique for recovering surface normals from multiple images of an object illuminated from different directions. Traditionally, photometric stereo methods rely on handcrafted features and assumptions about the lighting conditions. However, recent advancements in deep learning have opened up new possibilities for improving the accuracy and robustness of photometric stereo. In this literature review, we explore the state-of-the-art deep learning models applied to photometric stereo, discuss their strengths and limitations, and highlight potential future directions.
