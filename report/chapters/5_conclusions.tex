\section{Conclusions}
In conclusion, deep learning models have significantly improved the accuracy and robustness of photometric stereo. However, challenges remain, such as handling varying lighting conditions, generalizing to novel objects, and reducing computational complexity. Future research should focus on addressing these challenges and exploring novel architectures that combine the strengths of deep learning with the principles of traditional photometric stereo. As the field continues to evolve, we anticipate exciting developments that will enhance our understanding of 3D shape recovery and pave the way for practical applications in computer graphics, robotics, and augmented reality.
