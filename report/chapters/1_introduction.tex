\section{Introduction}
Photometric stereo is a fundamental computer vision technique that aims to recover the 3D shape of an object from its 2D images captured under varying lighting conditions. The traditional photometric stereo methods rely on assumptions about the lighting and surface reflectance properties, which limits their applicability to real-world scenarios. In recent years, deep learning models have shown promising results in various computer vision tasks, including photometric stereo. These models can learn complex representations directly from the data, allowing for more accurate and robust surface normal estimation. In this review, we delve into the recent advancements in deep learning-based photometric stereo and discuss their impact on the field.
